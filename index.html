A blocky 3D egg-heist adventure! Sneak into the rival bases, grab eggs, and bring them back to your blue base.

Mobile: left joystick to move, drag the right side to look, and tap STEAL near eggs. On desktop use WASD / arrow keys, mouse to look, Space to jump, E to steal.
